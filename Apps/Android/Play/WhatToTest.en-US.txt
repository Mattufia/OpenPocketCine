New and changed

- Pocket 3: an MT button beside the zoom chip turns Med-Tele, the 2× lens, on and off. The picture fades to black while the lens swaps.
- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) works in live view, video playback and photos. Choose a lens preset or a custom 1.00–2.00× factor in 0.01 steps.

Fixes

- Pocket 3: with Med-Tele on, the zoom chip offers 2×, 3× and 4×, and a 3× crop is no longer labelled as a lens.
- The assist palette no longer floats over the capture drawer or the gimbal panel.
- Improved recovery when a running preview loses its video format.

What to test

- Pocket 3: tap MT from 1× and from a digital crop, then tap it quickly several times. Check it lands on 2× or 1× and is dimmed while recording or in D-Log M.
- Tap DE-SQ to toggle; hold for presets, Custom and direction. Try LUT, grids, rotation and playback zoom. Check your custom factor survives a restart and files remain unchanged.
