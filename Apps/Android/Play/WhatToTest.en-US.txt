New and changed

- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) works in live view, video playback and photos. Choose a lens preset or a custom 1.00–2.00× factor in 0.01 steps.
- Report a problem is visible on the pairing screen, even before your first connection, with optional technical details and screenshots.

Fixes

- In portrait, the gimbal panel is only as tall as its controls instead of covering half the screen.
- Improved recovery when a running preview loses its video format.
- Live view starts again without the low-latency setting when the phone's decoder refuses it, instead of staying black.

What to test

- In portrait live view, open the gimbal panel. Check it fits its controls and leaves the rest of the picture visible.
- Tap DE-SQ to toggle; hold for presets, Custom and direction. Try LUT, grids, rotation and playback zoom. Check your custom factor survives a restart and files remain unchanged.
