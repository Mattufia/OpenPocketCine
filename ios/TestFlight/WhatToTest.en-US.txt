New and changed

- Pocket 3: an MT button beside the zoom chip turns Med-Tele, the 2× lens, on and off. The picture fades to black while the lens swaps.
- On Osmo Nano, zoom is hidden: no zoom chip, pinch zoom, hold disc or controller zoom. DISP settings list Zoom Chip and Gimbal Stick only when the camera has them.
- Osmo Action 6 support: live view, Normal 10-bit and D-Log M color with the official LUT, and an APERTURE control in place of FOCUS. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.

Fixes

- Pocket 3: with Med-Tele on, the zoom chip offers 2×, 3× and 4×, and a 3× crop is no longer labelled as a lens.
- Live video no longer stops shortly after connecting while the app is busy.
- In Auto exposure, SHUTTER shows the shutter the camera actually applied.

What to test

- Pocket 3: tap MT from 1× and from a digital crop, then tap it quickly several times. Check it lands on 2× or 1× and is dimmed while recording or in D-Log M.
- Connect a Nano: check there is no zoom chip and that pinch and controller L1/R1 and L2/R2 do not zoom. Switch to a Pocket and check zoom returns.
- If you have an Action 6: pair it, confirm live view appears, then try APERTURE, COLOR, Photo and Timelapse.
